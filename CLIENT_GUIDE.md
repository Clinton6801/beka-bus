## System Overview

### What is BEKA Bus Portal?

BEKA Bus Portal is a web-based registration and management system for BEKA Academy's learner transportation service. It allows:

- **Parents** to register their children, upload payment proof, and track registration status
- **Accounts Office Staff** to review registrations, confirm payments, and manage the system
- **Transparent Pricing** with multi-child discounts automatically calculated

### Key Features

✅ **Multi-Step Registration** - Simple form for parents to register multiple children  
✅ **Real-Time Fare Calculator** - Parents see exact costs before registering  
✅ **Secure Payment Proof Upload** - Parents upload receipt images/PDFs in the portal  
✅ **Staff Dashboard** - Accounts office reviews submissions and confirms registrations  
✅ **Email Notifications** - Automatic confirmations and status updates  
✅ **Route Management** - Flexible route and trip-type options  
✅ **Transparent Pricing** - All fees shown upfront; discounts for multiple children

## For Parents

### How to Register Your Child

#### Step 1: Access the Registration Form
1. Visit the BEKA Bus Portal home page
2. Click **"Start Registration Today"** button
3. You'll be taken to the registration form

#### Step 2: Enter Your Details (Step 1 of 4)
Fill in the following information:
- **Full Name** - Your name
- **Email** - Active email address (you'll receive status updates)
- **Phone Number** - Contact number
- **Address** - Your home address
- **Password** - Create a strong password (minimum 8 characters)
- **Confirm Password** - Re-enter your password

Click **"Next: Add Children"** to continue.

#### Step 3: Add Your Children (Step 2 of 4)
For each child, provide:
- **Full Name** - Your child's name
- **Class Level** - Their current class (e.g., Nursery 1, Primary 4)
- **Route** - Select their bus route from the dropdown
- **Trip Type** - Choose "One Way" or "Round Trip"

**You can add multiple children.** Click **"+ Add Another Child"** if you have more than one child.

The system automatically calculates a **multi-child discount**:
- 2 children: 10% discount on total fare
- 3+ children: 15% discount on total fare

Click **"Review & Submit"** when you've added all children.

#### Step 4: Review Your Information (Step 3 of 4)
- Verify all details are correct
- Make sure you've selected the right routes and trip types
- Click **"Next: Guidelines & Agreement"** to continue

#### Step 5: Accept Transportation Guidelines (Step 4 of 4)
- Read the BEKA Academy Transportation Guidelines and Code of Conduct
- Check the box: **"I have read, understood, and agree to abide by all transit safety policies, policy."**
- Click **"Submit Bus Service Application"**

#### Step 6: Your Reference Code
After successful submission:
- You'll see a **Reference Code** (e.g., BEKA-2026-6493)
- **Save this code** - you'll need it to check your status and make payment
- You can also click **"Check Status"** to view your registration anytime

### Making Your Payment

Once your registration is submitted:

1. **Payment Details:**
   - **Bank:** United Bank for Africa
   - **Account Name:** BEKA Academy
   - **Account Number:** 1023493204

2. **Payment Steps:**
   - Transfer the exact amount to the bank account above
   - **Important:** Use your **Reference Code** as the payment description/reference
   - This helps the school identify your payment

3. **Example:**
   - Child 1: ₦13,000 (Round Trip)
   - Child 2: ₦13,000 (Round Trip)
   - **Total:** ₦26,000 → 10% discount applied
   - **Final Amount to Pay:** ₦23,400

### Uploading Your Payment Proof

After making the bank transfer:

1. **Log in** to your parent portal at **BEKA Bus Portal → Parent Portal**
2. **Dashboard** shows your registered children
3. Find the child with **"Pending Review"** status
4. Scroll down to **"📄 Payment Proof"** section
5. **Upload your receipt:**
   - Take a photo of the bank receipt or screenshot
   - Drag the file into the upload box or click to select
   - Accepted formats: JPG, PNG, PDF (max 5MB)
   - **Include your Reference Code** in the receipt photo if possible
6. You'll see **"✓ Receipt Uploaded"** confirmation

### Tracking Your Status

#### Option 1: With Login
1. Go to **Parent Portal**
2. Log in with your email and password
3. Your dashboard shows all children's statuses

#### Option 2: Without Login
1. Go to **Check Status** on the home page
2. Enter your **Reference Code**
3. View registration status instantly (no login needed)

#### Status Meanings
- **🟡 Pending Review** - Your registration is submitted; awaiting staff confirmation
- **🟢 Confirmed** - Your registration is confirmed; your child is cleared for bus service
- **🔴 Rejected** - Your registration was not approved; contact accounts office for details
- **⚪ Expired** - This registration has expired; contact accounts office

## For Staff (Accounts Office)

### Accessing the Staff Portal

1. Visit the BEKA Bus Portal home page
2. Click **"Accounts Officer"** in the footer or navigate to the staff login
3. Log in with your staff account credentials
4. You'll see the **Registration Management Dashboard**

### Dashboard Overview

The dashboard shows:
- **Filter Buttons:** Pending | Confirmed | Rejected | All
- **Registration Table:** Lists all registrations with key details
  - Reference Code
  - Student Name
  - Route
  - Fare
  - Status badge
  - "⚠️ No receipt" indicator (if payment proof is missing)
  - Submitted date
  - "View Details" button

### Processing a Registration

#### Step 1: Review the List
1. By default, you see **"Pending"** registrations
2. Check if a **"⚠️ No receipt"** badge appears (parent hasn't uploaded proof yet)
3. Click **"View Details"** to open the full registration

#### Step 2: View Full Details
The detail page shows:
- **Student Information** - Name, class, route, trip type
- **Parent Information** - Name, email, phone, address (contact parent if needed)
- **Fare** - The total amount to pay
- **💳 Payment Receipt** - View the uploaded receipt (image or PDF)

#### Step 3: Confirm or Reject

**Option A: CONFIRM Registration**
1. Review the parent's payment proof
2. Verify the payment was received at the school bank account
3. Click **"✓ Confirm Registration"**
   - Status updates to "Confirmed"
   - Staff name and timestamp are recorded
   - Parent receives a confirmation email
4. Learner is now cleared for bus service

**Option B: REJECT Registration**
1. Click **"✗ Reject Registration"**
2. A text box appears: explain the reason for rejection
   - Example: "Receipt illegible" or "Partial payment received"
3. Click **"Confirm Rejection"**
   - Status updates to "Rejected"
   - Rejection reason is recorded
   - Parent receives an email with the reason

### Manual Refresh

To see newly uploaded receipts:
1. Click the **"🔄 Refresh"** button in the top-right
2. The dashboard reloads and shows the latest data
3. Any recently uploaded receipts will now be visible

### Fare Structure

Fares vary by route and trip type:
- **One-Way Trip:** ₦8,000 - ₦10,000+ (varies by route)
- **Round-Trip:** ₦15,000 - ₦18,000+ (varies by route)

### Multi-Child Discounts

- **1 child:** No discount
- **2 children:** 10% discount on total fare
- **3+ children:** 15% discount on total fare

**Example:**
- 2 children, Round-Trip each: (₦15,000 + ₦15,000) × 0.90 = ₦27,000

### Payment Terms

- Payment is **due after registration** is submitted
- Parents should use their **Reference Code** as the payment reference
- School staff will verify payment at the bank and confirm in the portal
- No physical proof is required once staff confirms payment

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Registration

**Q: How long does registration take?**  
A: The form takes 5-10 minutes to complete. Confirmation by staff typically takes 1-3 business days after payment proof is submitted.

**Q: Can I register multiple children at once?**  
A: Yes! Add as many children as needed in Step 2. The system will automatically calculate multi-child discounts.

**Q: Do I need to create a login to register?**  
A: Yes, you'll create a login during registration. This allows you to check your status and upload payment proof later.

**Q: What if I forget my password?**  
A: Contact the accounts office at accounts@beka.ng and provide your reference code. We'll help you reset it.

### Payment & Receipts

**Q: Do I need to print my receipt?**  
A: You'll upload a **digital** receipt (photo or screenshot of your bank transaction). No printed copy needed unless the school requests one.

**Q: How do I upload my receipt?**  
A: Log into your parent portal, find the pending registration, scroll to "Payment Proof," and drag/drop your receipt file. Accepted formats: JPG, PNG, PDF.

**Q: My receipt upload keeps failing. What should I do?**  
A: Check that:
- Your file is one of these formats: JPG, PNG, or PDF
- File size is under 5MB
- Your internet connection is stable
- Try a different browser if the problem persists

**Q: Why is my registration still showing "Pending Review"?**  
A: Staff may be waiting for:
- Payment proof to be uploaded
- Bank verification of payment
- More time to review (typically 1-3 business days)

**Q: What if the receipt is rejected?**  
A: The staff member will send you a rejection reason. Common reasons:
- Receipt is illegible or unclear
- Reference code not visible
- Partial payment (amount doesn't match)
- Payment not found in school bank records

Contact the accounts office for clarification.

### Status & Tracking

**Q: How do I check my registration status?**  
A: Two ways:
1. **Log in** to your parent portal and view your dashboard
2. **Check status** on the home page (enter Reference Code only, no login required)

**Q: What does "Confirmed" status mean?**  
A: Your registration and payment are approved. Your child is cleared for bus service this term.

**Q: What if my registration is "Rejected"?**  
A: Contact the accounts office immediately. They'll explain the reason and next steps.

### Technical Issues

**Q: The website won't load. What should I do?**  
A: Try these steps:
1. Refresh the page (F5 or Ctrl+R)
2. Clear your browser cache (History → Clear browsing data)
3. Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
4. Check your internet connection

**Q: I see an error message when I try to submit. What does it mean?**  
A: Common errors:
- **"Missing required fields"** - Fill in all fields marked with *
- **"Passwords do not match"** - Retype your password exactly the same
- **"Email already registered"** - That email is already in use; try logging in instead
- **"Invalid email format"** - Check your email spelling (e.g., name@example.com)

## Troubleshooting

### Parent Issues

#### Problem: Can't Log In
**Solution:**
1. Check your email and password spelling
2. Use the correct email you registered with
3. If you forgot your password, contact accounts office
4. Try a different browser

#### Problem: Receipt Upload Fails
**Solution:**
1. Check file format (JPG, PNG, or PDF only)
2. Reduce file size if it's over 5MB
3. Try uploading from a different device/network
4. Contact support if problem persists

#### Problem: Fare Amount Looks Wrong
**Solution:**
1. Verify you selected the correct route
2. Check if multi-child discounts applied (2+ children)
3. Confirm the trip type (One-Way vs Round-Trip)
4. Contact accounts office if it still seems incorrect

#### Problem: Status Not Updating
**Solution:**
1. Click refresh button or reload the page
2. Wait 1-2 business days for staff to confirm
3. Contact accounts office to check payment status

### Staff Issues

#### Problem: Can't See New Receipts
**Solution:**
1. Click the **"🔄 Refresh"** button
2. Reload the page (Ctrl+R or F5)
3. Try a different browser
4. Check your internet connection

#### Problem: Receipt File Won't Open
**Solution:**
1. Verify the file format (should be JPG, PNG, or PDF)
2. Try downloading the file and opening with a different program
3. Ask parent to re-upload a clearer version
4. Contact technical support

#### Problem: Can't Confirm or Reject Registration
**Solution:**
1. Ensure you're logged in with staff credentials
2. Verify the registration is in "Pending" status
3. Try a different browser
4. Contact technical support
